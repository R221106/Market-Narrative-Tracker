# Sentipulse

> **Market narrative intelligence through news, sentiment, keywords, trends, and sources.**

Sentipulse is a market narrative tracking dashboard that analyses financial news to help users understand **what the market is talking about, how the narrative is changing, and whether the overall sentiment is positive or negative**.

Instead of focusing only on stock prices, Sentipulse focuses on the **stories and narratives driving market attention**.

## Features

 **Financial News Aggregation**
  - Retrieve recent news for a selected market topic.

   **Sentiment Analysis**
  - Analyse overall sentiment surrounding a market narrative.
  - Analyse sentiment at individual article level.

   **Keyword Extraction**
  - Identify frequently occurring keywords within news articles.

   **Trend Analysis**
  - Analyse changes in news activity surrounding a topic.

  **Source Analysis**
  - Identify the major sources contributing to a market narrative.

   **Topic Watchlist**
  - Track predefined market topics such as AI, Nvidia, Bitcoin, Oil, and Tesla.

   **Popular Topics**
  - Retrieve popular topics stored by the application.

   **SQLite Database**
  - Store and retrieve relevant application data.

  **REST API**
  - Backend functionality is exposed through Flask API endpoints.
